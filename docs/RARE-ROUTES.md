# Rare routes and legal assumptions

All probabilities are fictional game balance. Named employers do not endorse the game. Thresholds describe game stats, not actual recruitment or immigration criteria.

| Route | Prerequisites before final attempt | Final chance |
|---|---|---|
| General Secretary | state path, North Korean residence, exceptional fictional succession reform, politics ≥90, loyalty ≥85, network ≥85 | .05% |
| Nintendo creator | resettlement, separately approved Japan work permission, Japanese residence, portfolio, tech ≥85, language ≥80, creativity ≥85, network ≥60 | .075% |
| Paris mayor | French residence, ≥5 years before naturalization application, approved French citizenship, Paris residence, council election, politics ≥90, language/network ≥85 | .05% |
| NYC mayor | permanent residence, ≥5 years before naturalization application, US citizenship, NYC residence, local campaign, politics ≥90, language/network ≥85 | .05% |

The reform event is explicitly alternate history; it is not a claim that ordinary North Korean engineers have a realistic succession path. It has its own .075% success probability. The joint leadership path is substantially rarer than a single .05% roll.

France: [CGCT LO2122-4-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006389917/2026-05-08) prevents non-French municipal councillors becoming mayor. The game models a council seat before a separate mayoral selection. NYC: [New York Public Officers Law §3](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/PBO/3) establishes age, US citizenship and local residence constraints. All final election events occur after age 50. [French naturalization guidance](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2213) informs the staged approach.

Law checked 2026-10-16; the game's later decades are speculative. Residence years alone do not confer citizenship. Language, admissibility, security screening, work authorization, electoral nomination and other detailed requirements are abstracted into approval events, never granted by winning the final job/election. Political eligibility is not a full legal simulator. Further jurisdiction-specific review is in BACKLOG.

Achievements persist independently of current job and retirement. Winning never ends a game. Final occupational ID and historical achievement can differ.

Tests prove every career has an executable producing transition and test all four rare successes with qualified fixtures and searched RNG states. These are **transition witnesses**, not claims that fixed-seed newborn playthroughs have been found for every rare path. Population reports record observed frequencies separately; a zero count is not proof of impossibility.
